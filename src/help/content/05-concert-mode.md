---
title: Concert mode
summary: The stripped-down on-stage view — paging, autoscroll, a silent beat pulse and band-wide sync.
---

Concert mode is a clean performance view for the stage. It shows one song at a time with big, readable text and nothing else in the way. It works on a phone, a tablet or a laptop.

## Starting it

- On a **setlist**, press the green **play** button. Concert mode plays the songs in setlist order.
- On a single **song**, press **Concert** in the Tools row.

![Concert mode.](concert)

## What's on screen

- **Header** — the song's place in the setlist (1/4), its title and artist, the capo if there is one, and your setlist note for the song.
- **The chart** — chords above the lyrics. Tap a chord to see its diagram, and tap again to dismiss it. Tabs and strumming patterns can be played right there too.
- **Your notes** — the notes you've typed on the song (see Notes in Practice tools) are shown on the chart. Only your own notes are shown, and they can't be edited here.
- **Up next** — in a setlist, the end of each song (its last page, or the bottom when scrolling) shows the next song. Tap it to go straight to that song.
- **Previous / Next** buttons at the bottom, with the **− A / + A** text-size buttons between them. You can hide Previous and Next under Preferences on your profile page.

## Moving between pages and songs

By default long songs are split into **pages**, which turn sideways like a book. The page you're on and how many there are (2/3) show at the top right. You can:

- tap the right half of the chart, or press **Next**, to go forward;
- tap the left half of the chart, or press **Previous**, to go back;
- use the left and right arrow keys on a keyboard, or press **Space** to go forward (with autoscroll on, Space starts and pauses the scrolling instead).

Going past the last page of a song takes you to the start of the next song.

With **Scroll** chosen under the cog, the song scrolls instead: tap the lower half of the chart to scroll down and the upper half to scroll up. Either way, the half you tapped flashes briefly.

If stray taps turn pages when you don't want them to, switch off **Tap to turn pages** under Preferences on your profile page. Tapping then doesn't turn pages; the other ways still work.

**Swipe left** to go forward and **swipe right** to go back: with pages that's a page at a time, carrying on into the next or previous song at either end (a song is always entered at its start); when scrolling it's a whole song at a time. The chart follows your finger with the next (or previous) page or song coming into view beside it; let go before it's far enough and it springs back. Swiping past the first or last song shows **Start of set** or **End of set**.

## Text size

**− A** and **+ A** make the text smaller or larger. Your size is remembered on this device.

## Quick actions

Tap the chart with **two fingers** to open a menu in the middle of the screen. The app's top bar (menu, profile and so on) is hidden in concert mode and appears while this menu is open. From there you can start or pause autoscroll, jump to the previous or next song, open the song list, **edit the song**, turn the **beat flash** and the metronome **click sound** on or off, and change the text size. The flash and click follow the song's BPM, so they only appear on songs that have one. The click is always off when you enter concert mode. Tap outside the menu to close it.

**Edit song** opens the song's edit page. When you save or cancel, you're taken back to concert mode on the same song.

The first time you open concert mode, a short introduction shows how to open the menu.

On a computer, press **M** to open the menu, and **M** or **Esc** to close it. With nothing open, **Esc** leaves concert mode.

With a mouse, push the pointer against the left edge of the screen to bring in the app's sidebar over the song. Move away from it and it slides back.

## Settings

Press the cog in the top right.

![Concert settings.](concert-settings)

- **Pages / Scroll / Autoscroll** — how the song is shown:
  - **Pages** (the default) — split into pages that you turn.
  - **Scroll** — one long page that you scroll yourself.
  - **Autoscroll** — one long page that scrolls by itself. Choosing this adds a speed slider.
- **BPM pulse** — a soft, silent flash at the song's tempo, so you can keep time without a click in your ears. The first beat of each bar flashes larger and in its own colour (amber unless you change it under **Profile → Preferences**), following the song's time signature (4/4 if none is set).
- **Solo / Follow / Lead** — see [Playing together](#playing-together).

All settings are remembered per device.

### Autoscroll

With autoscroll on, a green **down-pointing play** button appears at the top. Press it, or just **tap the chart**, to start and pause the scrolling.

- **Drag with one finger** (or use the mouse wheel) to scroll by hand, to look ahead or go back a bit. Autoscroll holds still while you do, then carries on from where you left it.
- **Drag with two fingers** to change the speed while it plays: down for faster, up for slower. A bar at the side shows the speed as you go.
- The speed can also be set from the cog.

## The song list

Press the list button to open the **Setlist Songs** panel and jump straight to any song in the setlist. Tap anywhere outside the panel to close it; that tap does nothing else, so it won't turn a page or start autoscroll.

![The setlist song list.](concert-navigator)

## Playing together

When you are playing a setlist, the cog also shows **Solo / Follow / Lead**:

- **Solo** — you navigate on your own.
- **Lead** — your navigation drives everyone else: song, page and transposition.
- **Follow** — your screen follows whoever is leading.

One person (the singer, say) picks **Lead** and everyone else picks **Follow**. The panel shows who is currently leading.

## Stopping

Press the red **stop** button. So that a stray tap on stage can't end the show, the first press only turns it into **Tap again**; press it a second time within a few seconds to leave concert mode.

> **Tip:** Run through your setlist once in concert mode before the gig to set text size, autoscroll speed and your sync role. Everything is remembered on that device.
