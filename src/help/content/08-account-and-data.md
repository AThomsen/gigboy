## Your profile

Open **Profile** from the top bar.

![Your profile and account settings.](profile)

- Change your **name**, **username** and **email**.
- Change your **password** (you need your current one), or set one if your account doesn't have one yet. Only shown if your Gigboy uses passwords.
- Add **passkeys** (if your Gigboy has them on), so you can sign in with your fingerprint, face or screen lock.
- **Log out** from the top of the page.
- Accept pending **band invites** under **Invites**.

### Signing in

Depending on how your Gigboy is set up, the sign-in screen offers some of these:

- **Password** — your email and password.
- **Email me a sign-in code** — you get an email with a 6-digit code and a link. Use this if you forgot your password or lost your passkey. **In the installed app, type the code** — the link opens in your browser instead of the app.
- **Sign in with a passkey** — uses a passkey saved on your device, or offered when you tap the email field. Add passkeys on your profile, one per device (or one that syncs through iCloud Keychain or Google Password Manager).

If you can't get into your email any more, ask whoever runs your Gigboy to change your email address.

### Preferences

The **Preferences** card holds settings that are saved to your account, so they apply on every device you sign in on.

- **Concert mode → Beat one colour** — the colour of the first beat of each bar in the concert-mode beat flash. Pick **Same as other beats** to turn the highlight off.
- **Concert mode → Previous / Next buttons** — whether concert mode shows the Previous and Next buttons at the bottom of the screen. Turn them off for more room if you turn pages by tapping, swiping or with a pedal.
- **Concert mode → Tap to turn pages** — whether tapping the chart turns the page (or, when scrolling without autoscroll, scrolls up or down). Tapping to start and pause autoscroll isn't affected. Turn it off if stray taps get in your way; swiping, the keys and the on-screen buttons still work.

## Storage

The bar under the band name in the sidebar shows how much storage the band has used. Click it for a breakdown of **recordings**, **images** and **attachments**. If you are running low, delete recordings or attachments you no longer need. The limit is set by whoever runs your Gigboy.

## Working offline

Gigboy is an installable app (see [Getting started](/help/getting-started)) and is built to keep working when you lose your connection, so it is a good idea to open tonight's setlist while you still have signal.

## Your data is yours

Under **Export your data** on your profile, press **Export as ZIP** to download everything: every song as a plain ChordPro file, plus recordings, press kit images, riders and gigs from all your bands. You can do this at any time, and the files open in any ChordPro-compatible app.

## Deleting things

Deleted songs, lists and press kits go to the **Trash** and can be restored for 30 days. Deleting your **account** (under **Danger zone** on your profile) permanently removes the bands you own and your profile data, so export first if you want to keep anything.
